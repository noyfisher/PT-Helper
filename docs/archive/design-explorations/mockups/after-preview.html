🆕 New Exercise
Wall Sits
Let's review the form before you start
▶ Animated demo
1
Setup
Stand with back flat against wall, feet shoulder-width apart, 2 feet from wall
2
Movement
Slowly slide down until thighs are parallel to ground (90° knee bend)
3
Return
Hold position, then slowly slide back up to standing
⚠️ Common Mistakes
✕
Knees past toes — move feet further from wall
✕
Lower back arching — press back firmly into wall
✕
Knees caving inward — push knees out over toes
Audio Tempo Pattern
Down 2 3 4 Hold 2 3 ... Up 2 3 4
