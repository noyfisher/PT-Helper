# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: July 2026**

This policy supplements our Privacy Policy and describes how we handle "consumer health data" as defined by the Washington My Health My Data Act and similar laws. It applies to all users.

## Health Data We Collect

- Health profile you provide: date of birth, sex, height, weight, medical conditions, medications and medication history, surgical history (including restrictions and hardware), and injury history
- Pain assessments: body regions, pain characteristics, aggravating/relieving factors, treatment history, and free-text notes
- Wellness assessments: goals, impact on daily activities, habits, and free-text notes
- Movement metrics derived on your device from form-check videos (the video itself never leaves your device)
- AI-generated outputs about your body: possible explanations, exercise plans, recovery insights, and form feedback

## Sources

All health data comes directly from you or is derived from data you provide in the app. We do not buy health data, collect it from other apps, or use advertising identifiers.

## Why We Collect It

- To generate your educational analysis, exercise plans, and progress insights
- To run safety checks on generated plans
- To let you track your own progress

We do not sell your health data, use it for advertising, or share it for marketing. We do not use geofencing.

## Who Can Access It

- Our service providers, only as needed to run the app: Google Firebase (authentication, storage), Anthropic (AI analysis), and OpenAI (independent exercise-safety checks)
- No one else, unless required by law

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** the health data we hold about you
- **Withdraw consent** to collection and sharing of your health data
- **Delete** your health data

To exercise any right: use Settings → Delete Account (removes all data immediately), or email noyfisher2003@gmail.com. We will respond within 45 days. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our response; if the appeal is denied, you may contact the Washington State Attorney General at www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.
