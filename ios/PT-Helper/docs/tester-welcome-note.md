# Welcome to the COIL test round 🌀

Thanks for helping us test **COIL** — an iOS app that turns a description of your aches, pains, or wellness goals into a personalized, AI-guided physical-therapy-style plan you can actually follow.

You're in our **first external test round** (v1.0, build 15). Your job is simple: **use it like it's real, then tell us where it felt confusing, slow, wrong, or broken.** Blunt feedback is the most useful kind — nothing is too small.

---

## Please read first (30 seconds)

- **COIL is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.** It's an experimental tool in testing. Do **not** use it to diagnose a condition or decide on treatment. For anything real, see a licensed clinician.
- **In an emergency, call your local emergency number** — do not use the app.
- **It asks for health information and consent.** You'll be shown Terms, a Privacy Policy, and a Health-Data consent screen on first launch — please read them; accepting is required to test. You can withdraw consent or delete your account anytime in Settings.
- **This is a test environment.** Feel free to enter realistic (but not sensitive) info. You can wipe everything with **Settings → Delete Account** when you're done.

## The 5-minute happy path

Please walk this end-to-end at least once:

1. Tap the center **➕ "Assess"** button → choose **Pain analysis**.
2. On the 3D body map, **rotate/zoom**, tap a sore area (e.g. lower back or a knee), pick 1–2 regions, **Continue**.
3. Fill in the pain form → **Apply to All Regions & Analyze**.
4. Review the **conditions** it suggests → tap **Build Rehab Plan**, pick your equipment/length/difficulty.
5. Open the plan → **Start Guided Workout** → complete a couple of sets, take a rest, finish or end early.
6. Peek at the **Progress** tab (charts, your last analysis) and **Plan** tab (saved plans).

Then poke around freely — try the **Wellness goals** path from ➕, exercise **swaps**, **Recovery Insights**, and the **Appearance** setting (Light/Dark).

## What we'd especially love your eyes on

- **Does the analysis feel trustworthy and relevant** to what you entered?
- **Are the exercises clear** — instructions, images, and the guided-workout flow (sets, rests, resume-after-closing)?
- **Onboarding & consent** — was anything confusing or annoying?
- **Anything that looks off** — broken images, wrong text, layout issues, dark-mode contrast, slowness.
- **Empty/offline states** — try turning on Airplane Mode before an analysis and see if the message makes sense.

## How to report something

Send reports to **[ feedback channel — email / Slack / TestFlight feedback — FILL IN ]**.

A great bug report has:
- **What you did** (the steps), **what you expected**, **what happened**
- A **screenshot or screen recording** if you can (in TestFlight you can shake the device to send feedback with a screenshot)
- Your **device + iOS version**, and roughly **when** it happened

Don't worry about duplicates or "is this a real bug" — send it anyway.
